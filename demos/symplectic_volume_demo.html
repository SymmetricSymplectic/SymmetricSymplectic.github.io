Liouville's theorem: phase-space volume is conserved

A blob of 420 initial conditions for the pendulum H(q,p) = p²/2 + (1 − cos q), evolved with two integrators. The leapfrog panel (symplectic) shears the blob along energy contours but never changes its area. Explicit Euler (not symplectic) lets the area drift — increase dt to see it blow up faster. The dashed circle is the initial blob; faint curves are level sets of H, the darker one the separatrix.
